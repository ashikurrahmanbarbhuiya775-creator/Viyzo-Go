# Viyzo Media Hub — Low-Cost Revenue Bootstrap

## Goal
Generate the first legitimate revenue before relying on platform ad monetization. This is a business workflow, not a promise of fast or guaranteed income.

## First offer: short-form video service for local businesses
Start with a small, repeatable service using footage supplied or approved by the client:
- 1 vertical 20–40 second promo/reel
- captions and on-screen business details
- one revision
- delivery as an MP4 for the client's own social account

Suggested pilot pricing (validate locally before publishing): ₹499–₹999 for a first simple video; quote more for filming, multiple revisions, voice talent, travel, or licensed stock. Collect a clear advance before work begins. Never promise views, virality, leads, or sales.

## Simple sales process
1. Choose one niche (restaurants, salons, tuition centres, repair shops, local stores).
2. Make one clearly labelled sample using original or client-authorized material; do not invent customer reviews or results.
3. Contact a small number of nearby businesses individually with a personal message and sample.
4. Confirm scope, language, delivery date, price, revision count, and usage rights in writing.
5. Collect advance, produce, get factual approval, deliver, and request permission to show the work in a portfolio.
6. Track outreach, paid orders, time spent, revisions, and net margin.

## Cost controls
- Keep paid AI providers disabled until the owner explicitly opts in and sets a provider-side budget/usage limit.
- Prefer client-supplied original footage and local FFmpeg editing where feasible.
- Use licensed, public-domain, or client-authorized media only.
- Do not store unnecessary source footage; apply retention and backup policies.
- Never treat free-tier quotas as guaranteed or unlimited. Check current provider limits before launch.

## Platform growth rules
- Publish consistently in the selected country's language and local context.
- Use truthful titles, thumbnails, captions, and relevant tags.
- Ask viewers genuine questions and reply to real comments.
- Review actual platform analytics (watch time, retention, shares, saves, follows) before changing strategy.
- Do not buy followers, automate fake engagement, spam groups, or promise a specific reach.
- Organic posts can travel beyond the selected country; country targeting/restrictions depend on each platform's supported controls and may not be absolute.

## Important limitations
- Facebook/YouTube monetization eligibility and payout timing are controlled by each platform and are not immediate or guaranteed.
- This playbook does not mean payment collection, invoicing, client CRM, Meta publishing, or analytics integrations are already connected.
- Obtain client permission for faces, premises, logos, music, and all supplied media.
